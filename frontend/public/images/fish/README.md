# Seafood illustrations

Copied unchanged from `superposition/reeldeal`, `apps/web/src/assets/fish/`,
as used in the local storefront at `http://127.0.0.1:4332/reeldeal/`.
Source asset provenance: commit `8218163160924ac78cb5a967988b5439336a392a`.

The six 1536 × 1024 WebP files are generated illustrations of katsuo, sanma,
saba, hotate, mebachi maguro and awabi on crushed ice. The source records
their refinement on 26 September 2026 using the built-in image generation tool.

They are presentation assets, not landing photographs or species evidence.
Submitted lot photographs take precedence. Unknown species retain the generic
silhouette. The workshop inventory and prices are synthetic previews.
